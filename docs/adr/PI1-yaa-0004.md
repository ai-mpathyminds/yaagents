# ADR PI1-yaa-0004 — Dual-license model (source-available / fair-code)

Status: [Accepted]
Date: 2026-05-17
PI: PI1-yaa
Deciders: yaagents-architect (license-threshold lock = chief-architect/user, OQ-1)

> **Disclaimer (verbatim, GTM README §Appendix):** This includes a draft
> licensing strategy for product planning. It is not legal advice. Before
> publishing the license publicly or accepting external contributions, consult
> a qualified software licensing lawyer.

## Context

YAAgents launches **source-available / fair-code**, NOT OSI open source, with a
dual Community (free) + Commercial license (PRD §8). OQ-1 asks whether the
Community threshold (employees / revenue / funding) is locked before legal
review.

## Decision

1. **Dual-license, source-available.** `LICENSE` = YAAgents Community License
   v0.1 (drafted from GTM README §14 verbatim); `COMMERCIAL.md` = commercial
   terms + contact. Marketing/README MUST use "source-available" /
   "fair-code" / "free for academics, individuals, small developers" —
   **never** "open source" / "OSI".
2. **Community threshold = combined AND gate**: `< 10 employees AND
   < USD 1,000,000 annual gross revenue` (GTM §13 recommendation). This is the
   *placeholder* the artifacts ship with.
3. **Threshold lock is chief-architect/user, not engineering (resolves OQ-1
   routing).** The number is a placeholder until chief-architect/user
   confirms before PI1-yaa **PC-6** (pre-publish gate). Engineering does NOT
   block on it; the artifacts ship with the combined-AND text and a
   `legal-review-pending` note carried verbatim.
4. **Legal review is a launch gate, not an engineering gate.** Accepting
   external contributions and public announcement are blocked on counsel sign-off
   (tracked in PRIORITIES / PC-6, not a WI acceptance criterion).

## Consequences

- WI-1yaa.REL-2 ships `LICENSE`, `COMMERCIAL.md` with the disclaimer verbatim
  and the combined-AND placeholder.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` carries a CLA placeholder + a "contributions not accepted
  until legal review complete" banner.
- PC-6 has a non-engineering checklist item: threshold-lock + counsel review.

## Alternatives considered

- *Single revenue-only or employee-only threshold* — deferred to the
  chief-architect/user lock; ADR records the combined-AND placeholder only.
- *Adopt an existing fair-code license (BSL/Elastic/SSPL) verbatim* — out of
  scope for the architect; GTM §14 draft is the directed source. Counsel may
  redirect at the legal gate.
