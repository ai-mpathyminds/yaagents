# YAAgents Commercial License

> This GTM README includes a draft licensing strategy for product planning. It is not legal advice. Before publishing the license publicly or accepting external contributions, consult a qualified software licensing lawyer.

---

## Who needs a commercial licence?

A commercial licence is required for any use **not** covered by the
YAAgents Community License v0.1 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). Specifically:

| Use case | Licence required |
|---|---|
| Individual developer / personal projects | Community (free) |
| Academic research, teaching, student projects | Community (free) |
| Non-commercial use | Community (free) |
| Evaluation / proof-of-concept | Community (free) |
| Organisation with **fewer than 10 employees** AND **less than USD 1,000,000 annual gross revenue** | Community (free) |
| Organisation with **10 or more employees** | **Commercial** |
| Organisation with **USD 1,000,000 or more** annual gross revenue | **Commercial** |
| Commercial SaaS, hosted, or managed service built on YAAgents | **Commercial** |
| Embedding YAAgents in a commercial product | **Commercial** |
| Consulting / SI / platform companies delivering paid services using YAAgents | **Commercial** |
| Any use that competes with a paid YAAgents offering | **Commercial** |

> **Threshold lock status:** The community threshold above
> (`<10 employees AND <USD 1,000,000 annual gross revenue`) is a
> combined-AND placeholder pending chief-architect/user confirmation at
> PI1-yaa PC-6. It is subject to change before the v0.1.0 public release.
> See [docs/adr/PI1-yaa-0004.md](docs/adr/PI1-yaa-0004.md).

---

## What the commercial licence provides

- The right to use YAAgents in production for commercial purposes as defined above.
- The right to embed or redistribute YAAgents as part of a commercial product or service.
- Priority support and SLA options (tiers TBD at commercial launch).
- The right to receive commercial release builds and security patches under the commercial channel.

The commercial licence is per-organisation. Sub-licencing to end users of
your product is included; reselling or redistributing the licence itself is
not.

4. Whether you need a trial period.
